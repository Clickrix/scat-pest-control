Service Plans

One Shot, Quarterly or Monthly

Pick the plan that fits your home — every plan is handled by skilled, licensed applicators.

1

One-Shot Service

Common pests for a one-shot service include bees, wasps, hornets, rodents, ants and other occasional invaders. One visit, problem solved.

2

Quarterly Service

Our most popular plan. We treat the entire perimeter of your home — no interior treatment or appointment needed — and provide no-charge return visits as needed. Bees, fleas, termites and bed bugs are not covered under this plan.

3

Monthly Service

Recommended for homes with a significant pest population — common in heavily wooded areas or near lakes, ponds and other bodies of water. A licensed applicator visits once a month to treat infestations and provide preventative maintenance.
